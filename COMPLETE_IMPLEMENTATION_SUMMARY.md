## 🚀 How to Use

### Landing Page:
1. Click "Health Tracker" from dashboard
2. See beautiful animated landing page
3. Click "Continue" to proceed or "Exit" to go back

### Create a Goal:
1. Go to "Goals & Risk" tab
2. Fill form:
   - Category: Weight/Bowel/Exercise/Sleep
   - Title: "Lose 5kg"
   - Target: 5
   - Unit: kg
3. Save → See progress bar

### View Risk:
- Automatically shown at top of Goals tab
- Color-coded (Green/Blue/Orange/Red)
- Shows warnings and recommendations
